# Notes — how this site is put together

Employer-facing on purpose. No political content — this site exists to
get support and analytics work, and everything on it points that way.

The portfolio page is the front door. Upwork is just the checkout behind
it: the services cards link out to the fixed-scope offers there, but the
first thing a hiring manager should see is this page, not a marketplace
profile.

Every project is labelled Live or Sample, honestly. Sample work says
sample, synthetic data says synthetic. Hiring managers do check — they
click the links — and the fastest way to lose a job is a claim that
doesn't survive one click. I'd rather lose on skills than on trust.
